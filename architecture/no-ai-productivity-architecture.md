## 1. Deterministic Productivity Engine vs Stochastic Models

Personal OS replaces stochastic, unpredictable AI prompts with **bulletproof mathematical algorithms**:

1. **Productivity Score Engine**:
   $$\text{Score} = \min\left(100, \left(\frac{T_{\text{done}}}{T_{\text{total}}} \times 40\right) + \left(\frac{H_{\text{done}}}{H_{\text{total}}} \times 30\right) + \left(\min\left(1, \frac{M_{\text{focus}}}{90}\right) \times 20\right) + 10\right)$$

2. **Event-Driven Background Scheduler**:
   - Zero-polling delta-timer min-heap queue in Electron Main.
   - Sleeps at 0.00% CPU until the exact microsecond an alert is due.
   - Reconciles missed reminders after Windows sleep/hibernate using deterministic time deltas ($\Delta t = t_{\text{resume}} - t_{\text{suspend}}$).

3. **Local SQLite Durability**:
   - Better-SQLite3 with Write-Ahead Logging (`PRAGMA journal_mode = WAL`).
   - 100% offline-first. Instant ACID transactions.
   - Automated periodic database backup with 1-click snapshot restoration.
